# Protocol Info

The packets appear to have a constant header / "Magic Bytes"  of
0xA31E1C


The data does not appear to mimic any known thermal printer standards (at least, unless it is encoded/encrypted).

I popped the data into Gemini which suggested it may be related to the "Nimbot" printers, but on looking at the Niimbluelib, the packet format appears to be different.

Gemini also suggested it may be similar to Xiaomi printers. But on checking this there appears to be no resources available for this brand online, however, a text search found Xiaomi listed in the source.
